01Executive Summary

Encore Cup worked as a field pilot. The district missed on goal because the conversion floor never held.

Encore Cup drove engagement and surfaced real performance signal — but the district missed its 1.80% conversion goal, closing at 1.40% across 32,210 transactions and 452 cards landed.

The diagnosis is not traffic. It is coaching and conversion floor. High-volume stores processed thousands of transactions while large segments of their cashier base landed zero applications across five full weeks. Among those who did land at least one, many could not sustain goal-level conversion.

Bottom line
Encore Cup worked as a diagnostic. What converts engagement into goal is a tiered coaching structure — not another blanket participation push.

06Insights

Three findings that reframe how the district should read Encore Cup results.

01

Missed on capability, not traffic

32,210 transactions produced 1.40% conversion. High-volume stores moved traffic but finished below goal because large portions of the floor never landed an application. Coaching must target the floor, not the register.

02

Zero-landed is the biggest drag

88 cashiers (40.6%) landed zero applications. 62 handled 20+ transactions; 43 handled 50+. At Avalon, 8 cashiers with 30+ txns each landed zero. Many had real opportunity.

03

Winners aligned; losers created false progress

Winners had smaller participation-to-competency gaps. Streets showed 64% landed vs 12% at goal — a 52-point leak. Activity-shaped outcomes without sustained performance.

07Recommendations

How the district moves forward — tiered coaching, a new scorecard, and a replicable store profile.

Tier A · Urgent

Zero + Exposure

Zero landed and 30+ transactions. 1:1 coaching, weekly check-in, manager ride-along.

Tier B · Skill

Landed, Below Goal

Proved conversion once. Pitch quality, close rate, and consistency coaching.

Tier C · Exposure

Low-Opportunity Zeros

Zero landed with <20 txns. Scheduling review before accountability conversation.

New district scorecard
Track % cashiers at goal, participation-to-competency gap, and zero-landed high-exposure count per store — weekly.
Crabtree Profile — district standard
≥55% landed ≥1 · gap ≤10 pts · ≤50% of cards from top 3 cashiers. Crabtree is the replicable template: broad participation with cards spread across the floor. Short Pump won on rate at 2.31%; the coaching opportunity is lifting the middle of the pack.

Next build: Add shift-count to weekly exports so Tier A/B/C assignment is based on confirmed opportunity, not transaction proxy alone.
